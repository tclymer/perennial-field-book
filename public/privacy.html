Privacy

Perennial Field Book · last updated 18 September 2026

Perennial Field Book is a free tool for mapping an orchard and keeping records about its trees, made by Threefold Farm. This page says what it stores and where.

Without an account

The app works with no account at all. Your farm is stored only in your own browser, on your own device. Nothing about it is sent anywhere. Map imagery is fetched from the provider you choose in Settings (Google, a public state or federal imagery service, or a URL you supply); those providers see the map tile requests, as any map does. There is no analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

When you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. It exists so that your other devices, and people you invite, can share one farm. When you sign in, the app's server stores:

  • your Google account's name, email address, and profile picture;
  • the records of any farm you choose to sync: its map, trees, notes, and photos;
  • which farms you belong to and who invited you.

Google is used only to confirm who you are. The app asks Google for nothing beyond your basic profile, never reads your Drive, mail, or anything else, and never posts to your account.

Who can see a farm

Everyone on a farm can see all of its records and who else is on it. The owner of a farm can remove a person or delete the farm from the server at any time; a member can leave. Access ends immediately, though copies already on someone's device stay there.

Where it lives and who runs it

The server runs on Cloudflare's network with data stored in the United States. It is operated by Threefold Farm as a volunteer effort, at no charge, with no guarantee of uptime or of keeping the service running indefinitely. Export a complete copy of your farm from Settings whenever you like; keep exports of anything you cannot afford to lose. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else, ever.

Deleting your data

Delete a farm from the server in Settings (owner) or leave it (member). To delete your account entirely, or with any other question, write to tim.clymer@gmail.com.
